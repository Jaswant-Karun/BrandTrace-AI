2. The Enterprise Governance Crisis & BrandTrace Promise

Modern enterprises publish thousands of digital ad variations, packaging labels, and partner rate cards monthly. When commercial parameters change (e.g., pricing revisions or medical classifications), organizations face cascading brand blindspots. Static PDF guidelines fail to alert in-flight campaigns, triggering Lanham Act lawsuits, FTC inquiries, and multi-million-dollar fines.

Trap 1: Trapped Truth-Sets

Approved commercial facts and claims live locked in PDFs and legal emails completely decoupled from marketing copywriters.

Trap 2: Silent Asset Invalidation

Mutating a single product claim or price silently turns hundreds of in-market ads deceptive without any system alert.

Trap 3: Unverifiable Approvals

Sign-offs lack cryptographic proof, exposing enterprises to strict liability when compliance audits investigate past releases.

The BrandTrace AI Value Proposition
"Know what your brand allows. Detect what your content risks. Understand the impact of changes. Approve with evidence. Preserve an auditable record."
