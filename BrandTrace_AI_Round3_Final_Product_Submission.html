1. Problem Statement & Market Opportunity

Modern consumer enterprises operate across dozens of distributed marketing channels, generating thousands of ad variations, press statements, packaging designs, and digital campaigns monthly. When commercial parameters change (product price revisions, regulatory healthcare classifications, or warranty terms), organizations suffer from cascading brand blindspots. Static PDF guidelines fail to alert teams of affected in-flight assets, leading to public customer deception, Lanham Act lawsuits, FTC Green Guide inquiries, and multi-million dollar regulatory fines.

Disconnected Truth-Sets

Approved prices, claims, and policies live trapped in silos separate from creative authoring and publishing tools.

Silent Asset Invalidation

Mutating a core commercial parameter silently renders hundreds of published ads out-of-date without warnings.

Unverifiable Approvals

Sign-offs lack tamper-evident provenance, creating severe compliance exposure during regulatory discovery.

4. Brand Knowledge Base & Truth-Set Management

The Brand Knowledge Base serves as the enterprise ground truth. Approved commercial facts, specifications, and regulatory guidelines are registered with formal evidentiary citations (e.g. "Aurelle Commercial Price Sheet Rev 4.1"), department owners, effective dates, restricted terms, and mandatory phrases. All records persist in MongoDB Atlas (approved_facts collection) and feature full-text and semantic vector indexing.

Approved Fact Schema (Sample)
{ "fact_id": "FACT-AUR-PRC-01", "name": "Cloud Studio Subscription", "category": "PRICE", "current_value": "₹499", "unit": "per month", "source_citation": "Price Sheet Rev 4.1", "restricted_terms": ["free tier", "unlimited beta"] }
Server-Side Safeguards
  • Duplicate submission prevention (idempotent fact registration).
  • ISO date validation (expiry_date >= source_date).
  • Server-side RBAC: Only BRAND_MANAGER or ADMIN can create or mutate facts.
  • Automatic bidirectional graph node registration in the BDIG.

5. Content Submission & Autonomous Multi-Agent Pipeline

When content is submitted through the Content Studio, the autonomous pipeline routes copy concurrently through 4 specialized agents, returning structured findings with token-level character offsets in under 800ms:

1. Voice & Persona Agent

Scans for informal slang, unauthorized tone shifts, and channel mismatch. Scores tone consistency (0-100) with span annotations.

2. Claim Substantiation Agent

Extracts numerical, performance, and pricing claims. Matches against MongoDB approved facts. Identifies unsubstantiated assertions.

3. Regulatory & Legal Compliance Agent

Cross-references statutory regulations (FDA 21 CFR §870, FTC 16 CFR §260.4, Lanham Act §43(a)). Flags critical statutory non-compliance.

4. Cascading Blast Radius Agent

Traverses the NetworkX directed acyclic graph to highlight downstream marketing touchpoints and active campaigns at risk.

9. Security, Authentication & Reliability Hardening

Enterprise Security Controls
  • Salted Password Hashing: PBKDF2 with SHA-256 cryptographic salts and constant-time equality verification.
  • Sliding-Window Rate Limiter: Enforces 120 req/min per IP/token with standard Retry-After headers.
  • Server-Side RBAC: Enforces 5 roles (ADMIN, BRAND_MANAGER, CREATOR, REVIEWER, COMPLIANCE_REVIEWER).
Data Sanitization & Validation
  • Input Boundaries: 50,000 character maximum payload ceiling (HTTP 413 for oversized submissions).
  • File Upload Defense: Rejects executable/script files (.exe, .sh, .py, .bat) and 0-byte uploads.
  • Credential Redactor: Strips MongoDB connection strings, JWT secrets, and AI API keys from all error logs.

13. Known Limitations & Architectural Boundaries

  • Render Free-Tier Spindown: Backend instances spin down after 15 minutes of inactivity; initial cold-start wake-up takes ~30-45 seconds.
  • Zero-Trust Offline PWA Policy: Live content inspections, price simulations, and release signing require internet connectivity and are not cached locally to avoid validating against stale compliance policies.
  • Deterministic Rule Fallback: If external Gemini API quotas are exhausted, the system seamlessly falls back to deterministic rule matrices (NetworkX, token extractors, statutory lookup tables) ensuring 100% uptime without hallucinations.
  • Logical Multi-Tenancy: Partitioned logically at the software and database index level via organisation_id rather than physical per-tenant database clusters.

14. Responsible AI & Governance Guardrails

Zero Hallucination Architecture

AI findings are strictly bound to verified statutory regulations and customer-approved facts. When unverified claims are detected, the system explicitly cites the missing evidence rather than guessing.

Mandatory Human Sign-Off

Autonomous agents suggest remediations and compute risk scores; only authorized human brand managers and compliance officers hold the authority to approve releases or commit policy changes.
